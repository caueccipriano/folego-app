## Description
Your balance is not necessarily what you can spend.

Fôlego organizes your finances to show what is truly free today, considering upcoming commitments, your budget, and expected income.

With Fôlego you can record income and expenses, track accounts and cards, organize recurring expenses and subscriptions, plan category limits, track goals, review upcoming commitments, use financial reminders, and keep a financial journal.

Advanced features may require a Premium subscription, including future projections, automations, statement import, and data export.

You can delete your account and associated data directly in the app.

Fôlego is not a bank, payment institution, investment advisory service, or credit service. Projections and indicators are personal financial organization tools and depend on the data you provide.
