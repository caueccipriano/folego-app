## Full description
Your balance is not necessarily what you can spend.

Fôlego organizes your finances to show what is truly free today, considering upcoming commitments, your budget, and expected income.

With Fôlego you can:

- record income and expenses;
- track accounts, cards, benefits, and debts without mixing balances and credit limits;
- organize recurring expenses and subscriptions;
- plan category limits;
- track financial goals;
- see upcoming commitments;
- use essential financial reminders;
- keep a financial journal for decisions and context.

Fôlego is designed to reduce the feeling of an endless spreadsheet: record what matters and get a clearer view of your month and available money.

Advanced features may require a Premium subscription, including future projections, automations, statement import, and data export.

Your financial data stays linked to your account, and you can request deletion of your account and associated data directly in the app.

Fôlego is not a bank, payment institution, investment advisory service, or credit service. The information shown is intended for personal financial organization.
